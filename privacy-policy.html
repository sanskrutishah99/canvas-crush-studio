Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2024

1. Who We Are

Canvas Crush Studio ("we", "our", "us") operates canvascrushstudio.com. We curate artworks and publish research-backed writing on colour psychology, with recommendations that may include affiliate links to partner retailers. This policy explains how we handle your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name and email address — when you sign up for our newsletter or send us a message through our contact form
  • Message content — anything you write to us via the contact form, so we can respond
  • Browser data — standard analytics (pages visited, time on site) to improve your experience

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your messages and enquiries
  • To send our newsletter (only if you opted in — you can unsubscribe anytime)
  • To improve our website and curated collections
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Cookies

We use cookies to remember your preferences. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share information only with:

  • Formspree — our form provider, which relays your contact-form messages and newsletter signups to us

When you follow an affiliate link, the partner retailer's own privacy policy applies on their site. We never pass your personal data to affiliate partners.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing emails at any time
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, leave us a message.

7. Data Retention

Contact-form messages are kept only as long as needed to respond. Newsletter subscriber data is kept until you unsubscribe. You may request earlier deletion by contacting us.

8. Security

Our site is served over industry-standard SSL encryption. We do not collect or store payment information — all purchases happen on our partner retailers' sites.

9. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, leave us a message via our contact form and we'll get back to you by email.
